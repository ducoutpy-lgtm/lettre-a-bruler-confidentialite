“Letter to Burn” (Lettre à brûler) app — effective November 17, 2026

1. The app

“Letter to Burn”, published by ARCADIA (Évry, France; contact@arcadiaco.fr), lets you write a letter that burns without ever being saved, follow a guided breathing exercise, and throw or find anonymous messages (“Message in a Bottle”). By using the app, you accept these terms.

2. Adults only

The app is for people aged 18 and over. Message in a Bottle is only available after you confirm your age.

3. Not a treatment

The app is a wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose anything and does not replace a doctor, a psychologist or an emergency service. If you are in distress, call or text 988 (United States), call Samaritans on 116 123 (United Kingdom), 3114 (France) or your local emergency services.

4. Message in a Bottle: content rules

A message you throw is read by one single stranger. It is anonymous and cannot be taken back. You must not include:

  • information that could identify anyone (name, phone number, address, email, social media);
  • hateful, discriminatory, threatening, harassing or violent content;
  • sexually explicit content, or any content involving minors;
  • encouragement of suicide, self-harm or dangerous acts;
  • advertising, links, scams or repeated messages;
  • any illegal content or content that infringes the rights of others.

By throwing a bottle, you expressly agree that its text, which may reveal personal or sensitive information (for example about your health or your feelings), is hosted and passed on to an unknown reader, as described in the Privacy Policy.

5. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Every bottle you open can be reported, and you can choose to stop receiving bottles from the same person. ARCADIA reviews reports and may, without notice, delete a message and stop a phone from throwing new bottles if these rules are broken. For any complaint: contact@arcadiaco.fr.

6. Responsibility

Each author is responsible for the content of their bottles. ARCADIA is not their author and cannot guarantee that no message will be upsetting, but acts promptly once illegal content is reported. The app is provided as is; the bottle service may be interrupted or changed.

7. Ownership

The app, its texts, sounds and illustrations belong to ARCADIA or to their authors (photo credits in the app). You keep your rights to your messages and allow them to be passed on to a reader as part of the service.
