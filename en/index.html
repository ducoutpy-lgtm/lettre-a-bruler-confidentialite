Privacy Policy

“Letter to Burn” (Lettre à brûler) app — effective November 17, 2026

In short. The letters you burn never leave your phone and are never saved. Only “Message in a Bottle” uses the internet: your messages travel under an anonymous ID, without your name, email or location. On our servers, the text of a bottle that has been read is erased 2 days after it is read. A small non-personalized ad banner (removable with a one-time purchase), no analytics. We do not sell or share your personal information.

Who is responsible for your data

The app is published by ARCADIA (Évry, France). For any question about your data: contact@arcadiaco.fr.

1. The letter to burn

The text you write only exists while you are writing it. It is never saved (not on your phone, not anywhere else), never sent over the internet and never kept: it disappears when the letter has burned. Nobody, not even the publisher, can read it.

2. Breathing, reminders and settings

Your settings (breathing rhythm, sound, vibration, reminder time) are stored only on your phone. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself: nothing is sent.

3. Message in a Bottle

This is the only part of the app that exchanges data with our hosting provider.

  • An anonymous ID, generated at random for your phone, is created the first time you visit an island. It contains no name, email or phone number.
  • When you throw a bottle: the text of your message (1,000 characters maximum), your anonymous ID, the island in the app you threw it from (a place in the app, not your real location) and the date.
  • When you walk along a shore: the bottles that were offered to you, linked to your anonymous ID.
  • When you open a bottle: your anonymous ID as its reader, the island and date of the find; and, if you report it, the fact that it was reported.
  • When you report a bottle or choose to stop getting its author’s bottles: the link “this anonymous ID no longer receives bottles from that one”, without ever revealing the author.
  • If the terms of use are broken: the anonymous ID of the phone concerned may be added to a list of banned phones.

What it is used for. Washing your bottle up on someone else’s shore, letting you know it was found (and on which island), letting the reader report it, and preventing abuse (a daily limit of bottles per phone). Nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no selling.

Who can read your message. One single person, a stranger to you, who finds it by chance. They do not know who wrote it. You do not know who read it. Do not put names, phone numbers or addresses in a bottle.

How long.

  • Text of a bottle that has been read, and its reader’s ID: erased from our servers 2 days after it is read. The person who read it may, however, have chosen to keep it in their collection, on their own phone.
  • What remains of a bottle that has been read (the author’s anonymous ID, islands, dates, status “found” or “reported”): deleted 6 months after it was read. This lets us tell you it was found.
  • Bottle never found: deleted 6 months after it was thrown.
  • Bottles offered on a shore: erased after 24 hours.
  • Blocks (“stop getting their bottles”): deleted after 12 months.
  • Banned phone: kept for as long as its anonymous ID exists.
  • Anonymous ID: deleted after 12 months without any use of Message in a Bottle, once no bottle is linked to it.

These deletions are automatic and run every hour.

A bottle that has been thrown cannot be taken back, just like a real message in a bottle.

Where. Bottles are hosted by Supabase, on servers located in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase acts on our behalf (data processing agreement under Article 28 of the GDPR) and does not use this data for itself. Like any server, it may briefly record technical connection data (such as the IP address) for security purposes.

Transfers outside the European Union. Although bottles are stored in Ireland, Supabase (whose contracting entity is based in Singapore) and some of its own providers, located in particular in the United States, may access technical data for support, security and the proper running of the service. These transfers are covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable).

4. What stays on your phone

Your phone keeps your settings, the bottles you chose to keep, bottles thrown without a connection while they wait to leave (erased as soon as they are sent), the list of bottles already opened, reported or that you were told about (IDs only, no text) and, if you use Message in a Bottle, your anonymous connection ID. Everything is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4a. Advertising and the “no ads” purchase

Advertising. A small Google AdMob banner appears only on the home screen and the island choice — never while writing, burning, breathing or next to the help numbers. Ads are non-personalized and limited to general-audience content; none is requested before you answer Google's consent form (Europe and United Kingdom). To show and measure the ad, limit repetition and fight fraud, Google receives technical data: IP address, device model and settings, interactions with the ad, and may use cookies or identifiers according to your choices. The app has removed access to the Android advertising ID. Google never receives the text of a letter or a bottle, nor your activity in the app. Google processes this data under its own policies (policies.google.com). You can change your choices at any time: Settings › Ad choices.

“No ads” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we receive neither your bank details nor your identity. The app only checks with Google Play, on your phone, that the purchase was made. No purchase data is sent to our servers.

5. Security

Exchanges between the app and our hosting provider are encrypted (HTTPS). The database is locked: no phone can read it directly; only a few specific operations are allowed, and none of them reveals the ID of an author or a reader. A bottle can only be opened by one person, to whom it was offered on a shore. The number of bottles thrown and opened is limited per day and per phone.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and the UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Because the app knows neither your name nor your email, we cannot link a request to your bottles; in any case they are erased within the time limits above. For any question: contact@arcadiaco.fr. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or, in the United Kingdom, to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

United States residents. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You may contact us at the address above to exercise any privacy rights available under the law of your state.

7. Audience

The app is intended for adults (18 and over).
